## What we use

This website uses no advertising, targeting or third-party analytics cookies.

Visitor statistics are collected with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, does not fingerprint devices and does not track individuals across sites. It records aggregate page views and performance data only.

The site stores two things in your browser, neither of which identifies you:

- **Wholesale client confirmation.** If you confirm you are a wholesale client to view the wholesale strategies, that confirmation is kept for the current browser session only and is cleared when you close the browser.
- **Preferences.** Your browser may keep minor display preferences (such as a chosen filter) so the page behaves the same on your next visit.

## Third-party services

Two forms on this site are provided by third parties: the contact form (Formspree) and the email subscription form (Mailchimp). These providers may set their own cookies when you use those forms. Their handling of your information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Links from this site to the SS&C fund registry, Olivia123 and other external sites take you to services we do not operate. Their cookie practices are their own.

## Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not affect your use of this site, except that you may be asked to confirm your wholesale client status again.
